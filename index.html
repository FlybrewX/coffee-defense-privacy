Coffee Defense is a game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. We do not collect any personal data.

What data the app collects, how, and why

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send gameplay or device data to us. Apple processes in-app purchases as described below.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (cleared stages, stars, coffee beans, unlocked heroes, Mastery levels and achievements) and preferences on your device.

In-app purchases

Coffee Bean packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. The game uses App Store purchase details to add the beans you bought and keeps recent transaction IDs on your device to prevent duplicate delivery.

Data retention and deletion

We do not receive or retain your game progress. You can clear game progress or remove the app's local data at any time:

  • In the game: Settings → Reset progress clears game progress. The game keeps preferences and recent purchase transaction IDs.
  • On your device: deleting the app removes its local data, including saved preferences and transaction IDs.

Consent and your choices

Because the game does not collect or process personal data, there is no consent to give or revoke. If you have a question or want to make a request about your data, email us at the address below and we will reply.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

We do not share any user data with third parties: the game includes no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or other data-collecting services. If that ever changes, any such third party will be required to provide the same or equal protection of user data as described in this policy, and this policy will be updated first. Payments for in-app purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
